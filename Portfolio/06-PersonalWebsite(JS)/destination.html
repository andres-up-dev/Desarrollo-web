New York City

I've been there twice and it never stops impressing me. Walking through Central Park and seeing the skyline from the top of the Empire State is something else.

  • Province/State: New York
  • Country: United States
  • Population: 8.5 million
  • Latitude & longitude: 40.7128, -74.0060

Los Angeles

Hollywood and Venice Beach might be the clichés, but the food scene alone makes it worth a visit. Plus the weather is amazing year round.

  • Province/State: California
  • Country: United States
  • Population: 3.9 million
  • Latitude & longitude: 34.0522, -118.2437

Manzanillo

Some of the best seafood I've ever had. It's a big port but the beaches are laid back and the sunsets are incredible.

  • Province/State: Colima
  • Country: Mexico
  • Population: 194,690
  • Latitude & longitude: 19.0522, -104.3158

Perula

A tiny fishing village on the coast of Jalisco where you can actually unplug. No crowds, just quiet beaches and good food.

  • Province/State: Jalisco
  • Country: Mexico
  • Population: ~1,000
  • Latitude & longitude: 19.5947, -105.1108

Chapala

When I need a quick weekend escape near Guadalajara, Chapala is the first thing that comes to mind. The malecon is perfect for a long walk.

  • Province/State: Jalisco
  • Country: Mexico
  • Population: 55,000
  • Latitude & longitude: 20.2966, -103.1905
